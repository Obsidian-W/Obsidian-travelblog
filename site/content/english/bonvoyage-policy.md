**Last updated: 13 November 2026**

BonVoyage is a free, non-commercial, open-source travel-planning application. It is designed to work entirely offline and does not collect personal data.

## Data collection

BonVoyage does **not** collect, transmit, or share your personal information. It does not require an account and does not use analytics, advertising, tracking technologies, or third-party tracking SDKs.

## Data stored on your device

Information you enter into BonVoyage remains on your device and is used solely to provide the application's features. BonVoyage does not send this information to a developer-operated server.

If you export information, you control where the exported file is saved or shared. Opening or sharing exported data with another service, including spreadsheet applications or cloud-storage providers, is subject to that service's own privacy practices.

## Internet access

BonVoyage's core functionality works without an internet connection. The app does not automatically upload your trips or other app data.

## Payments and advertising

BonVoyage is non-commercial. It does not display ads, sell personal data, or offer paid tracking-based features.

## Children’s privacy

BonVoyage does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users, because it does not collect personal information at all.

## Open-source code

BonVoyage is open source. You can inspect the source code and report issues directly in the [BonVoyage GitHub repository](https://github.com/Obsidian-W/Bon-Voyage). If you visit GitHub, GitHub’s own privacy practices apply.

## Changes to this policy

If the app's privacy practices change, this policy will be updated and the revision date above will be changed.

## Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact options available on [Obsidian Travels](https://obsidiantravels.net/) or the [BonVoyage GitHub issue tracker](https://github.com/Obsidian-W/Bon-Voyage/issues). This policy describes the **BonVoyage app**, not the privacy practices of the Obsidian Travels website.
