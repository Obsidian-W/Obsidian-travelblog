Obsidian Travels is operated by Timothé Paris. This policy explains what information may be collected when you visit the site or contact me.

## Hosting and technical data

The site is hosted by Netlify. Like most hosting providers, Netlify may process technical information needed to deliver and secure the site, such as your IP address, browser and device information, requested pages, timestamps, and diagnostic or security logs. See [Netlify's privacy policy](https://www.netlify.com/privacy/) for details.

## Contact form

If you use the form on the About page, the information you submit—such as your name, email address, and message—is processed so I can read and reply to your request. The form is handled through Netlify Forms. Do not include sensitive personal information in your message.

I keep messages only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain relevant correspondence, or meet legal obligations. I do not sell contact details.

## Comments

Article comments are provided by Disqus. Loading or using the comment section may allow Disqus to process identifiers, cookies, device information, and activity according to the [Disqus privacy policy](https://help.disqus.com/en/articles/1717103-disqus-privacy-policy). You do not need to use Disqus to read the articles.

## Advertising and Google services

The site may use Google AdSense to display advertising. If advertising is enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or other identifiers to serve, measure, and personalize ads where legally permitted. Learn [how Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

Where required, you will be asked for consent before non-essential advertising or analytics cookies are used. You can also control cookies through your browser settings and any consent controls displayed on the site.

## External links and embedded content

Articles may link to or embed content from third-party services, including YouTube, Wikimedia Commons, Instagram, LinkedIn, and travel or transport providers. Those services have their own privacy policies and may receive technical data when you visit them or load their content.

## Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To make a request, use the contact form on the [About page](/about/). I may need enough information to verify and answer the request.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the site's services or legal obligations change. The latest revision date will always appear at the top of this page.
