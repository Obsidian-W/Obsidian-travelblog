## Day 1: Budapest

![Hungarian Parliament](images/blog/hungary1.jpg)

Start at the **Hungarian Parliament** and Kossuth Square, then walk along the Danube to the **Shoes memorial**. From the river, head inland to **St Stephen's Basilica**, whose dome overlooks Pest. Continue through **Deák Ferenc tér** to the **Great Synagogue** on Dohány Street and the Jewish Quarter around Kazinczy Street.

The Parliament's guided interior visit covers the main staircase and the hall containing the Hungarian crown. **Tram 2** follows the Pest embankment, with views across to Castle Hill.

## Day 2: Budapest

![Vajdahunyad Castle](images/blog/hungary2.jpg)

Take **bus 16 from Deák Ferenc tér** to **Castle Hill**. Visit **Matthias Church** and the arcades of **Fisherman's Bastion**, then walk south through the old streets to **Buda Castle** and its terraces above the Danube.

Return by bus 16 to Deák Ferenc tér, then take **Metro Line 1 to Hősök tere** for **Heroes' Square**, **Vajdahunyad Castle** and **City Park**. The castle combines several architectural styles around a courtyard and lake. **Széchenyi Baths** are beside the park, with indoor thermal rooms and large outdoor pools. [Book Széchenyi admission here (affiliate link)](https://www.getyourguide.com/budapest-l29/szechenyi-spa-full-day-entrance-pass-t4139/?partner_id=H1N6CGH&utm_medium=online_publisher).

**Gellért Baths** have been closed for renovation since October 2025.

## Day 3: Danube Bend

Take the morning train from Budapest-Nyugati to **Esztergom**. Visit the basilica, treasury and castle museum, then walk down to the Danube and across the Maria Valeria Bridge for the view back from Slovakia.

The **880–889 bus corridor** follows the river from **Esztergom** through **Visegrád** and **Szentendre**. In Visegrád, walk uphill from the riverside road to the **Upper Castle** for its terraces overlooking the bend in the Danube.

Continue by bus to **Szentendre**. Walk between **Fő tér**, the Serbian churches and the riverside promenade, then take **HÉV H5** back to **Batthyány tér** in Budapest.

## Day 4: Kosice and Poprad

![Central Kosice](images/blog/slovakia.jpg)
![A church in Kosice](images/blog/slovakia1.jpg)

Take the morning train from **Budapest-Keleti to Košice**, roughly **3:30**. I used it as a stop rather than a full day: leave the bag at the station, walk along Hlavná to St Elisabeth Cathedral, the Urban Tower, singing fountain and State Theatre, then return for the train to **Poprad-Tatry**.

I continued to Poprad and used it as the base for both mountain days. You could stay in Košice instead and travel to Poprad the following day, adding a night before the hikes.

The train from **Košice to Poprad-Tatry** takes around **1:15**. The **Tatra Electric Railway** leaves from the upper platforms: roughly **an hour to Štrbské Pleso** or **0:25 to Starý Smokovec**.

## Day 5: Mount Rysy

![Strbske Pleso in the High Tatras](images/blog/slovakia2.jpg)

Take the Tatra Electric Railway from Poprad-Tatry to **Štrbské Pleso**. Follow the red trail to **Popradské pleso**, then the blue and red markers towards **Chata pod Rysmi** and **Mount Rysy**. The return hike is around **22 km**, with **1 250 m of ascent** and roughly **8:30 on foot**. Chains and metal steps cross the steeper section below the hut; above it, the trail climbs to the border ridge overlooking Poland. The upper route closes from **1 November to 31 May**.

## Day 6: High Tatras

![A trail in the High Tatras](images/blog/slovakia3.jpg)

Take the electric railway to **Starý Smokovec**, then the funicular to Hrebienok. Walk between Rainerova Hut, the waterfalls and Zamkovský Hut before returning to Hrebienok.

I walked all the way down instead of taking the funicular. The descent was steep and not especially interesting. The funicular is the better return to Starý Smokovec.

Return by electric railway to **Poprad-Tatry**, then take the evening main-line train to **Bratislava hlavná stanica**, around **four to five hours**.

## Day 7: Bratislava

![The Blue Church in Bratislava](images/blog/slovakia4.jpg)

Walk through Michael's Gate, the main square and the old streets below **Bratislava Castle**. Descend to the **Danube promenade**, then walk east to the **Blue Church** on Bezručova Street. Its pale-blue façade, roof and tiled details are quite different from the buildings around the main square.

Take the direct regional train from **Bratislava hlavná stanica to Wien Hauptbahnhof**, around **an hour**. **U1** runs from the station to **Karlsplatz** and **Stephansplatz** in central Vienna.

## Day 8: Vienna

![Karlskirche in Vienna](images/blog/vienna1.jpg)
![Belvedere Palace](images/blog/vienna2.jpg)

Walk the **Ringstrasse** from the Opera past the **Hofburg**, Parliament and Rathaus. Turn into the centre for **St Stephen's Cathedral**, then follow Graben and Kohlmarkt back towards the Hofburg courtyards.

Take **tram D to Schloss Belvedere** for the palace gardens and the Upper Belvedere collection, including Klimt's *The Kiss*. **Karlskirche**, with its two carved columns and green dome, is west of the palace on the walk back towards Karlsplatz. [Upper Belvedere admission is available here (affiliate link)](https://www.getyourguide.com/vienna-l7/vienna-art-and-architecture-in-the-belvedere-t47912/?partner_id=H1N6CGH&utm_medium=online_publisher).

## Day 9: Vienna

Take the U4 to **Schönbrunn** for the palace rooms, gardens and Gloriette. Return on the same line to **Kettenbrückengasse** for **Naschmarkt**. Walk past the **Secession Building**, with its gold-leaf dome, to the **MuseumsQuartier**. The **Leopold Museum** has works by Schiele and Klimt; across Maria-Theresien-Platz, the **Kunsthistorisches Museum** has the larger old-master collection.

Schönbrunn and one of these museums take most of the day, with the market between them.

Vienna bored me more than the other stops, even with two days there.

## With more days

### More of the High Tatras

From **Hrebienok**, continue past **Zamkovský Hut** into **Malá Studená dolina** to **Téryho Hut**, beside five mountain lakes at **2 015 m**. Allow around **five to six hours return from Hrebienok**, plus breaks. Another lake walk starts at **Biela Voda**, reached by bus from Tatranská Lomnica, and follows the valley to **Zelené pleso** and its lakeside hut, around **five hours return**.

### Budapest with a separate bath afternoon

Spend the morning at the **Museum of Fine Arts**, beside Heroes' Square, for its Egyptian objects, classical sculpture and European painting. Walk through **City Park** afterwards and keep the afternoon for **Széchenyi**. **Metro Line 1** links the park with central Pest.

### Another night in Košice

Visit the **East Slovak Museum**, at the northern end of Hlavná, for the **Košice Gold Treasure**: coins, medals and a Renaissance chain discovered during building work in the city. Continue through the side streets around **Mikluš Prison** and **Hrnčiarska**, with its restored craft workshops, before returning to the cafés around the theatre.
