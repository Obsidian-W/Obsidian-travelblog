## Day 1: Budapest

![Hungarian Parliament](images/blog/hungary1.jpg)

Start at the **Hungarian Parliament**, walk along the Danube past the Shoes memorial and continue to St Stephen's Basilica. Cross the centre through Deak Ferenc ter to the Great Synagogue and the Jewish Quarter. Tram 2 covers the Pest embankment when the walk becomes repetitive.

## Day 2: Budapest

![Vajdahunyad Castle](images/blog/hungary2.jpg)

Take Bus 16 to **Castle Hill** for Matthias Church, Fisherman's Bastion and Buda Castle. Return to Pest on Metro Line 1 for Heroes' Square, Vajdahunyad Castle and the paths through City Park. Finish in Széchenyi Baths, or choose Gellért if the day ends on the Buda side.

## Day 3: Danube Bend

Take the morning train from Budapest-Nyugati to **Esztergom**. Visit the basilica, treasury and castle museum, then walk down to the Danube and across the Maria Valeria Bridge for the view back from Slovakia.

Board the southbound regional bus to **Visegrád** and get off for the climb or shuttle to the Upper Castle. Continue on the same Danube road by bus to **Szentendre** for the lanes between the main square and the river. HÉV line H5 returns from Szentendre to Batthyány tér in Budapest.

## Day 4: Kosice and Poprad

![Central Kosice](images/blog/slovakia.jpg)
![A church in Kosice](images/blog/slovakia1.jpg)

Take the morning train from Budapest-Keleti to **Košice**. I used it as a stop rather than a full day: leave the bag at the station, walk along Hlavná to St Elisabeth Cathedral, the Urban Tower, singing fountain and State Theatre, then return for the train to **Poprad-Tatry**.

Sleeping in Košice also works, but it shortens the following day's Rysy hike. I continued to Poprad and used it as the base for both mountain days.

## Day 5: Mount Rysy

![Strbske Pleso in the High Tatras](images/blog/slovakia2.jpg)

Take the Tatra Electric Railway from Poprad-Tatry to **Štrbské Pleso** and follow the marked trail towards Popradské pleso and Chata pod Rysmi. The return hike to **Mount Rysy** fills the day and includes chains on the steeper upper section. Start with the first practical mountain train and check that the high trail is open before leaving Poprad.

## Day 6: High Tatras

![A trail in the High Tatras](images/blog/slovakia3.jpg)

Take the electric railway to **Starý Smokovec**, then the funicular to Hrebienok. Walk between Rainerova Hut, the waterfalls and Zamkovský Hut before returning to Hrebienok.

I walked all the way down instead of taking the funicular. The descent was awful: steep enough to be tiring but not interesting enough to justify it. Take the funicular down and save your knees for the walk to the station.

Board the evening train towards Bratislava.

## Day 7: Bratislava

![The Blue Church in Bratislava](images/blog/slovakia4.jpg)

Walk through Michael's Gate, the main square and the old streets below **Bratislava Castle**. Cross to the Danube promenade, then use a tram or bus for the **Blue Church** before returning to the station.

Take the direct regional train from Bratislava hlavná stanica to Vienna.

## Day 8: Vienna

![Karlskirche in Vienna](images/blog/vienna1.jpg)
![Belvedere Palace](images/blog/vienna2.jpg)

Walk the Ringstrasse between the Opera, Hofburg, Parliament and Rathaus, then cross the centre to St Stephen's Cathedral. Take tram D to **Belvedere** for the palace gardens and museum.

## Day 9: Vienna

Take the U4 to **Schönbrunn** for the palace rooms, gardens and Gloriette. Return on the same line to Kettenbrückengasse for **Naschmarkt**, then walk through the MuseumsQuartier and finish around the Hofburg streets missed on Day 8.

Vienna bored me more than the other stops, even with two days there.
