## Day 1: Stockholm

![Stockholm](images/blog/sweden1.jpg)

Begin in **Gamla Stan** around Stortorget, the Royal Palace and Stockholm Cathedral. Cross towards Riddarholmen, then follow the waterfront past City Hall and return through Norrmalm. The metro handles the longer jump between the accommodation and the old centre.

## Day 2: Stockholm

Take the metro to Brommaplan and Bus 176 or 177 to **Drottningholm Palace**. Visit the palace, theatre grounds and lakeside gardens, then return to Stockholm and walk the viewpoints along **Monteliusvägen** in Södermalm.

## Day 3: Stockholm

Take the tram or ferry to **Djurgården**. Visit the Vasa Museum first, then continue to the Nordic Museum and spend the rest of the day in **Skansen** among the relocated historic buildings and workshops.

## Day 4: Uppsala

![Uppsala](images/blog/sweden2.jpg)

Take a regional train from Stockholm Central to **Uppsala**. Walk between the cathedral, Gustavianum and the university quarter, then take a local bus to **Gamla Uppsala** for the royal burial mounds and museum. Return to central Uppsala and continue by train to Örebro.

## Day 5: Orebro

Walk around **Örebro Castle**, then follow the river towards the open-air streets of **Wadköping**. Continue through Stadsparken before taking the westbound train to Gothenburg.

## Day 6: Gothenburg

![Gothenburg](images/blog/sweden3.jpg)

Walk from the central station along the canals to the city museum and the old streets around Kronhuset. Continue through Haga and climb Skansen Kronan, then return by tram through the centre. The tram network covers almost every useful move within Gothenburg.

## Day 7: Gothenburg archipelago

Take Tram 11 to **Saltholmen** and use the public ferries into the southern archipelago. Choose two neighbouring islands, such as Styrsö and Donsö, and connect them by bridge or ferry. The same local transport ticket covers the tram and the regular passenger boats.

## Day 8: Malmo

Take the direct train south to **Malmö**. Walk from the station through Stortorget and Lilla Torg to Malmöhus Castle, then continue through the parks and along the waterfront towards the Turning Torso. Cross the Öresund by train to Copenhagen in the evening.

## Day 9: Copenhagen

![Rosenborg Castle in Copenhagen](images/blog/denmark1.jpg)

Start at **Rosenborg Castle** and the King's Garden, then walk through the centre to Amalienborg and Frederik's Church. Continue along the harbour to Nyhavn and cross the inner harbour for the evening around Christianshavn.

## Day 10: Copenhagen

![Central Copenhagen](images/blog/denmark2.jpg)
![Copenhagen from above](images/blog/denmark3.jpg)

Visit **Christiansborg Palace**, including the royal reception rooms and tower, then walk past the old stock exchange and along the canals. Continue to the National Museum and City Hall Square before departure.
