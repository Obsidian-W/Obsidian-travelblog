Eleven days from **Bali to Jakarta**, crossing Java through Ijen, Bromo, Yogyakarta and the Dieng Plateau.

Before crossing Java, add the **Gili Islands** for beaches and diving or fly to Labuan Bajo for a short **Komodo National Park** boat trip. These need additional days before crossing to Java.

## Day 1: Ubud

Start at **Ubud Palace** and the market, then walk south to the **Sacred Monkey Forest**. Return through the centre for Saraswati Temple and the Campuhan Ridge Walk towards sunset. [Perama's shared shuttles](https://peramatour.com/b/en/shuttle-bus) connect Bali airport and several coastal towns with Ubud; accommodation pickup is another option.

I hated Bali. The parts I saw were dirty and overwhelmed by overtourism, foreign-owned shops, tattoo businesses, prostitution and drug dealing. Ubud felt detached from the rest of Indonesia I saw afterwards.

## Day 2: Ubud

![Goa Gajah near Ubud](images/blog/indo1.jpg)

Take a small-group excursion or arrange a driver from Ubud for **Goa Gajah** and **Tegenungan Waterfall**. At Goa Gajah, the cave entrance is carved as an enormous open mouth; the grounds also contain bathing pools and stone statues. Tegenungan lies farther south, with a stairway down to the waterfall and river.

Return via **Pura Samuan Tiga**, close to Goa Gajah, for its gateways and courtyards. [Ubud temple and waterfall excursions are available here (affiliate link)](https://www.getyourguide.com/ubud-l32246/day-trips-tc172/?partner_id=H1N6CGH&utm_medium=online_publisher).

## Day 3: Ubud

![A temple in Bali](images/blog/indo2.jpg)

Spend the morning north of Ubud at **Tegallalang's rice terraces** and **Tirta Empul**, whose spring feeds the temple's purification pools.

Return to Ubud for the road transfer to **Gilimanuk**, arranged through the accommodation, around **four to five hours**. Cross to **Ketapang** on the public ferry, roughly **0:45–1 hour**, then continue into **Banyuwangi** for the night. Java is **one hour behind Bali**.

## Day 4: Ijen

![Ijen crater](images/blog/indo4.jpg)

Use a hostel-arranged shared vehicle to **Paltuding**, the **Ijen** trailhead, before dawn. The path climbs around **3 km** through forest to the crater rim. Return to **Banyuwangi** after sunrise, then take a train from **Banyuwangi Kota or Ketapang to Probolinggo**, around **four to five hours**.

Continue from **Probolinggo's Bayuangga bus terminal** by minibus to **Cemoro Lawang**, roughly **1:30–2 hours**. These minibuses wait until enough seats are occupied, so late arrival can mean paying for the empty places with the other passengers.

## Day 5: Bromo

![Mount Bromo and its caldera](images/blog/indo3.jpg)

Leave before dawn for the sunrise viewpoint above **Mount Bromo**. Walk back through Cemoro Lawang, descend into the Sea of Sand and cross to the staircase at the crater rim. Return to **Probolinggo** after the hike and take an afternoon or evening train west to **Yogyakarta**, around **seven to eight hours**. Services arrive at **Yogyakarta/Tugu** or **Lempuyangan**, both near the centre.

## Day 6: Yogyakarta

Visit the **Kraton**, the sultan's palace, in the morning for its open pavilions and royal collections. Walk south-west to **Taman Sari**, the former royal bathing complex, and through the surrounding residential lanes. Continue to Beringharjo Market and Malioboro. Trans Jogja buses cover the railway station and the main roads through the centre.

## Day 7: Borobudur

![Borobudur](images/blog/indo6.jpg)

Take the bus from Jombor terminal towards **Borobudur**, or use a shared hostel transfer for the early entry slot. Book the [temple-structure ticket](https://ticket.borobudurpark.com/) to climb **Borobudur**; the grounds-only ticket does not include the monument. Its lower galleries carry carved Buddhist narratives, while the upper terraces are lined with bell-shaped stupas.

[Borobudur guided visits and transfers are available here (affiliate link)](https://www.getyourguide.com/borobudur-l88720/?partner_id=H1N6CGH&utm_medium=online_publisher).

Visit **Pawon** and **Mendut** on the return towards Yogyakarta. Mendut's main chamber contains three large Buddhist statues; the two smaller temples lie east of Borobudur and need local transport between them.

## Day 8: Prambanan

![Prambanan](images/blog/indo5.jpg)

Take **Trans Jogja bus 1A** from Malioboro to **Prambanan Terminal**, then walk to the temple entrance. The tallest temples are dedicated to Shiva, Vishnu and Brahma; reliefs around the galleries tell episodes of the Ramayana. Continue north inside the archaeological park to **Candi Sewu**, a Buddhist complex surrounded by rows of smaller ruined shrines. Back in Yogyakarta, use the remaining evening for the southern square and the food stalls around Malioboro.

## Day 9: Temanggung and Dieng Plateau

From **Yogyakarta's Jombor terminal**, travel by bus via **Magelang to Wonosobo**, then change for a local minibus up to **Dieng**. Allow roughly **six to seven hours**, including the changes. Visit the **Arjuna temple complex**, whose small Hindu shrines stand against the volcanic hills, then the steaming **Sikidang crater** and **Telaga Warna**. Stay in Dieng after the visit.

Covid left me in the Temanggung area for weeks. I sorted tobacco, helped harvest rice and taught English and French, but that was not part of the intended itinerary. With more time, spend two or three days between Temanggung and Dieng rather than forcing the plateau into a single day.

## Day 10: Semarang

Take a bus from Wonosobo towards **Semarang**, via Temanggung. From the terminal, use Trans Semarang or a booked ride into Kota Lama, then walk around **Gereja Blenduk**, the domed church in the old Dutch quarter, and the streets around Taman Srigunting. Continue to **Lawang Sewu**, the former railway company's headquarters, for its long arcaded corridors and stained-glass windows. Add Sam Poo Kong by Trans Semarang bus or booked ride if the bus from Temanggung arrives early.

## Day 11: Jakarta

Take an early train from **Semarang Tawang to Jakarta Gambir**, around **five to six hours**. From Gambir, city buses reach **Kota Tua**; the commuter railway does not stop at Gambir. Walk around **Fatahillah Square**, then continue to Sunda Kelapa harbour. Finish around Merdeka Square with **Monas**, Istiqlal Mosque and Jakarta Cathedral. One day was enough for the parts of Jakarta I wanted to see.

## With more days

### Dieng: another two nights

Stay in **Dieng** for the pre-dawn walk up **Sikunir**, above the village of **Sembungan**, then visit the lake viewpoints at **Batu Ratapan Angin**. Another morning can cover the smaller temples around the Arjuna complex and the **Kailasa Museum**, before returning downhill by minibus to Wonosobo.

### More time in Yogyakarta

Go southeast to **Kotagede**, the former Mataram capital, for its old market, mosque, royal cemetery and silver workshops. Farther south, **Imogiri** contains the royal tombs of the Yogyakarta and Surakarta dynasties above a long stone staircase. An accommodation-arranged day trip can connect the two; access inside the tomb enclosures follows limited visiting days and local dress requirements.

### Islands before Java

Add several days before crossing to Java for the **Gili Islands**, or a flight to **Labuan Bajo** and a boat trip in **Komodo National Park**. Return to Bali before the westbound transfer to Gilimanuk.
