## Day 1: Taipei

Start at **Longshan Temple**, with its carved stone pillars, roof dragons and incense-filled courtyards. Walk through Wanhua to **Bopiliao Historic Block**, a row of restored brick shop houses, then follow the old streets north towards the Presidential Office area. Continue to **Ximending** for the evening.

---

## Day 2: Taipei

Walk through **Dihua Street**, where the old shop houses sell dried foods, tea and traditional medicines, and continue to the **Dadaocheng riverfront**. Take the metro to **Chiang Kai-shek Memorial Hall** for its broad square and gardens. In Xinyi, walk through **Zhongshan Park** around Sun Yat-sen Memorial Hall, then finish at **Raohe Night Market**, beside Songshan station.

---

## Day 3: Jiufen and Shifen

![A themed Taiwan Railways train](images/blog/taiwan1.jpg)

Take a Taiwan Railways train from Taipei Main Station to **Ruifang**. Buses leave from outside Ruifang station for **Jiufen**. Walk through the old street, climb to the temple viewpoints and continue beyond the busiest covered section before returning to Ruifang.

Use the Pingxi railway line for **Shifen Old Street** and walk from the station to **Shifen Waterfall**. Return to Ruifang on the same branch line.

Take an east-coast train from **Ruifang to Hualien** rather than returning to Taipei. The journey takes roughly **1:30–2:00** on an express service that stops at Ruifang.

## Day 4: Hualien

Start at **Pine Garden**, above the coast, then walk down to the seafront and follow it south towards **Beibin Park**. Turn inland through the old railway streets to the **Hualien Cultural and Creative Industries Park** on Zhonghua Road. The former wine-factory buildings now hold exhibitions, craft shops and performance spaces.

Return east through the centre for **Dongdamen Night Market**, where the food stalls spread across several adjoining market streets. The coast, old railway area and former factory can be linked on foot.

---

## Day 5: Qixingtan

Take **bus 308 from Hualien Bus Station to Qixingtan**. Walk the long pebble beach, continue along the cycle path and visit the small **Chihsing Tan Katsuo Museum** area before returning to Hualien. The same bus links the railway station, Dongdamen Night Market and Qixingtan.

---

## Day 6: Taroko

![Taroko Gorge](images/blog/taiwan2.jpg)

I visited **Taroko Gorge** by bus from Hualien. At the time, the bus stopped through the gorge and made it possible to get off for the trails, then continue on a later service.

The through-gorge bus service remains suspended following the **2024 earthquake**. In October 2026, **bus 310 from Hualien** and **bus 302 from Xincheng Station** still ended at **Taroko Visitor Centre**.

From the Visitor Centre, the open **Dekalun–Dali Village trail** climbs through forest to the hillside settlement. Allow **three to four hours return** for Dali, then take the bus back to Hualien. The old **Shakadang**, **Swallow Grotto** and **Tunnel of Nine Turns** walks remain closed; Dali is a mountain walk above the entrance area.


Take a **Taiwan Railways express from Hualien to Kaohsiung** via **Taitung**, around **four hours**. Services also stop at **Xin Zuoying**, with a Red Line connection into central Kaohsiung.

---

## Day 7: Kaohsiung

![The Dragon and Tiger Pagodas in Kaohsiung](images/blog/taiwan3.jpg)

Take the local train or metro connection towards Zuoying and visit the temples and pavilions around **Lotus Pond**. Walk between the Dragon and Tiger Pagodas area, Spring and Autumn Pavilions and the northern temples before returning to central Kaohsiung. End at **Liuhe Night Market**.


---

## Day 8: Kaohsiung

Use the light rail for **Pier-2 Art Center** and the harbour, then continue to Gushan ferry pier. Take the short ferry to **Cijin Island**, walk through the market street and climb towards the lighthouse and old fort before following the coast to the beach.

Continue to **Taichung** after the harbour visit. **Taiwan Railways expresses** take around **three hours** between the central stations. The **high-speed train from Zuoying to Taichung HSR** takes roughly **an hour**, followed by a local train from **Xinwuri** into central Taichung.

---

## Day 9: Taichung

![Rainbow Village in Taichung](images/blog/taiwan4.jpg)

Start at **Rainbow Village**, then return towards the centre for the old railway-station area, **Taichung Park** and the National Taiwan Museum of Fine Arts. Finish at **Fengjia Night Market**.

**Rainbow Village** is a small former military settlement in Nantun, painted with bright figures and patterns by Huang Yung-fu. **Buses 56 and 617** serve the village. Back in the centre, walk between the old station and **Taichung Park**, then continue by city bus to the **Fine Arts Museum** and **Fengjia Night Market**.


---

## Day 10: Sun Moon Lake

![Cycling beside Sun Moon Lake](images/blog/taiwan5.jpg)

Take [Nantou Bus 6670](https://ntbus.com.tw/hsr6.html) from Gancheng in central Taichung or Taichung HSR Station to **Shuishe**, at Sun Moon Lake. The bus takes around **1:30–2:00**. Rent a bicycle in Shuishe and follow the lakeside path south past Shuishe Dam to the **Xiangshan Visitor Centre**. The protected Shuishe–Xiangshan section is about four kilometres each way.

Return the bicycle in **Shuishe** after the ride.


---

## Day 11: Sun Moon Lake

Take the round-the-lake bus from Shuishe to **Wenwu Temple**, then continue to Ita Thao. Walk the Ita Thao lakeside trail and use the ropeway for the view above the eastern shore. Continue by bus to Xuanzang Temple and climb from there to **Ci'en Pagoda**.

Return to **Shuishe**, take **bus 6670 to Taichung HSR**, then the high-speed train to **Taipei Main Station**. Allow roughly **three to four hours including the change**.

---

## Day 12: Taipei

Take the metro to Shilin and bus R30 to the **National Palace Museum**. Its painting, calligraphy, bronze and jade galleries take at least three hours. [National Palace Museum admission is available here (affiliate link)](https://www.getyourguide.com/taipei-city-l190/taiwan-national-palace-museum-e-ticket-t249007/?partner_id=H1N6CGH&utm_medium=online_publisher). Return to Shilin, continue by metro to **Xinbeitou** and walk through Beitou Park to the Hot Spring Museum, Thermal Valley and the public baths.

---

## Day 13: Taipei

Take the metro to **Taipei 101/World Trade Center** and walk around Xinyi before climbing the Elephant Mountain steps for the skyline view. Return to Taipei Main Station for the airport MRT.

## With more days

### Tainan between Kaohsiung and Taichung

Add two nights in **Tainan** before heading north. The old centre includes the **Confucius Temple**, **Chihkan Tower** and small temple lanes; **Anping Fort** and the old trading waterfront make a separate western outing.

### Another night at Sun Moon Lake

Walk the **Maolan Mountain Trail**, starting near **Mingtan Junior High School**, north of Shuishe. The path climbs through tea fields towards the weather station and views over the lake. Keep another afternoon for the quieter lakeside paths around **Toushe** or another stretch of the cycling route beyond Xiangshan.
