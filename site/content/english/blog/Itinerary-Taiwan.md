## Day 1: Taipei

Start at **Longshan Temple**, with its carved stone pillars, roof dragons and incense-filled courtyards. Walk through Wanhua to **Bopiliao Historic Block**, a row of restored brick shop houses, then follow the old streets north towards the Presidential Office area. Continue to **Ximending** for the evening.

The airport MRT links Taoyuan Airport with Taipei Main Station. In the city, the metro covers almost everything on this first day.

---

## Day 2: Taipei

Walk through **Dihua Street**, where the old shop houses sell dried foods, tea and traditional medicines, and continue to the Dadaocheng riverfront, then take the metro to **Chiang Kai-shek Memorial Hall**. Continue by metro to Sun Yat-sen Memorial Hall and finish at **Raohe** or another night market on the same side of the city.

---

## Day 3: Jiufen and Shifen

![A themed Taiwan Railways train](images/blog/taiwan1.jpg)

Take a Taiwan Railways train from Taipei Main Station to **Ruifang**. Buses leave from outside Ruifang station for **Jiufen**. Walk through the old street, climb to the temple viewpoints and continue beyond the busiest covered section before returning to Ruifang.

Use the Pingxi railway line for **Shifen Old Street** and walk from the station to **Shifen Waterfall**. Return to Ruifang on the same branch line.

Take an east-coast train from **Ruifang to Hualien** rather than returning to Taipei. Select a service that stops at Ruifang in the [Taiwan Railways timetable](https://www.railway.gov.tw/tra-tip-web/tip/tip001/tip112/querybytime?lang=EN_US) and reserve a seat.


---

## Day 4: Hualien

Visit **Pine Garden**, then walk down towards the coast and the old railway area before crossing the centre to **Dongdamen Night Market**. Local buses cover Hualien station, the centre and seafront; the market is easiest to reach on foot from central accommodation.

---

## Day 5: Qixingtan

Take bus 308 from Hualien Bus Station to **Qixingtan**. Walk the long pebble beach, continue along the cycle path and visit the small Chihsing Tan Katsuo Museum area before returning to Hualien. The same bus links Hualien station, Dongdamen Night Market and Qixingtan; the [national iBus system lists its current departures](https://www.taiwanbus.tw/eBUSPage/Query/QueryResult.aspx?lan=E&rn=1606880571477&rno=03080).

---

## Day 6: Taroko

![Taroko Gorge](images/blog/taiwan2.jpg)

I visited **Taroko Gorge** by bus from Hualien. At the time, the bus stopped through the gorge and made it possible to get off for the trails, then continue on a later service.

This day cannot currently be repeated in the same form. The 2024 earthquake badly damaged the gorge. Buses 310 and 302 currently only reach the Taroko Visitor Centre; the Visitor Centre–Tianxiang bus service remains suspended. Bus 310 leaves Hualien via Qixingtan and Xincheng; bus 302 starts at Xincheng Station. The park lists the [open sections and bus routes](https://www.taroko.gov.tw/en/listicle/faq/534).


Take the Taiwan Railways service south from Hualien through Taitung to **Kaohsiung** after the visit.

---

## Day 7: Kaohsiung

![The Dragon and Tiger Pagodas in Kaohsiung](images/blog/taiwan3.jpg)

Take the local train or metro connection towards Zuoying and visit the temples and pavilions around **Lotus Pond**. Walk between the Dragon and Tiger Pagodas area, Spring and Autumn Pavilions and the northern temples before returning to central Kaohsiung. End at **Liuhe Night Market**.


---

## Day 8: Kaohsiung

Use the light rail for **Pier-2 Art Center** and the harbour, then continue to Gushan ferry pier. Take the short ferry to **Cijin Island**, walk through the market street and climb towards the lighthouse and old fort before following the coast to the beach.

Taiwan Railways trains connect central Kaohsiung directly with **Taichung**. High-speed trains require transfers through Zuoying and Taichung HSR stations.

---

## Day 9: Taichung

![Rainbow Village in Taichung](images/blog/taiwan4.jpg)

Start at **Rainbow Village**, then return towards the centre for the old railway-station area, **Taichung Park** and the National Taiwan Museum of Fine Arts. Finish at **Fengjia Night Market**.

**Rainbow Village** is a small former military settlement in Nantun, painted with bright figures and patterns by Huang Yung-fu. It is outside the centre; [Taichung's visitor transport map](https://www.taichung.travel/file/1549/) lists buses 56 and 617 for the village. For the rest of the day, the old station and Taichung Park are within walking distance of each other; the Fine Arts Museum and Fengjia require separate bus journeys.


---

## Day 10: Sun Moon Lake

![Cycling beside Sun Moon Lake](images/blog/taiwan5.jpg)

Take [Nantou Bus 6670](https://ntbus.com.tw/hsr6.html) from Gancheng in central Taichung or Taichung HSR Station to **Shuishe**, at Sun Moon Lake. Rent a bicycle in Shuishe and follow the lakeside path south past Shuishe Dam to the **Xiangshan Visitor Centre**. The protected Shuishe–Xiangshan section is about four kilometres each way.

Return the bicycle in Shuishe and stay at the lake. The [Sun Moon Lake administration describes each section of the cycle route](https://www.sunmoonlake.gov.tw/EN).


---

## Day 11: Sun Moon Lake

Take the round-the-lake bus from Shuishe to **Wenwu Temple**, then continue to Ita Thao. Walk the Ita Thao lakeside trail and use the ropeway for the view above the eastern shore. Continue by bus to Xuanzang Temple and climb from there to **Ci'en Pagoda**.

Return to Shuishe, take the Nantou Bus to Taichung and continue by Taiwan Railways train to Taipei. The [Nantou Bus Sun Moon Lake page](https://www.ntbus.com.tw/shuttle.html) carries the current shuttle information.

---

## Day 12: Taipei

Take the metro to Shilin and bus R30 to the **National Palace Museum**. Its painting, calligraphy, bronze and jade galleries take at least three hours. Return to Shilin, continue by metro to **Xinbeitou** and walk through Beitou Park to the Hot Spring Museum, Thermal Valley and the public baths.

---

## Day 13: Taipei

Take the metro to **Taipei 101/World Trade Center** and walk around Xinyi before climbing the Elephant Mountain steps for the skyline view. Return to Taipei Main Station for the airport MRT.

Check the [official Taiwan Railways search](https://www.railway.gov.tw/tra-tip-web/tip/tip001/tip112/querybytime?lang=EN_US) for current intercity schedules.
