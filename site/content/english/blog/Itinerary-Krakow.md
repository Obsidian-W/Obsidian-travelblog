## Day 1: Krakow

![Central Krakow](images/blog/krakow3.jpg)

Walk from the **Barbican** through **St Florian's Gate** and Floriańska Street to **Rynek Główny**. **St Mary's Basilica** faces the square, with its unequal towers and the carved wooden altarpiece inside. The **Cloth Hall** runs across the middle of the square, with shops beneath the arcades. Cross the square to Grodzka and follow the old royal route past the churches of Saints Peter and Paul and Saint Andrew to **Wawel Castle**.

Visit Wawel Cathedral and the castle courtyards, then leave the hill beside the dragon statue. Follow the river to the Father Bernatek footbridge and cross into **Podgórze** for Ghetto Heroes Square. Return across the same bridge into Kazimierz, passing the Old Synagogue, Szeroka Street and Plac Nowy. This is roughly eight kilometres from the Barbican to Plac Nowy.

## Day 2: Auschwitz

![Auschwitz-Birkenau Memorial](images/blog/krakow2.jpg)

Take a morning train from **Kraków Główny** to Oświęcim. The memorial entrance at 55 Więźniów Oświęcimia Street is about 20 minutes on foot from the railway station; local buses also stop outside it.

Reserve the named entry pass through [the official Auschwitz-Birkenau booking site](https://visit.auschwitz.org/) before the trip. The standard guided visit lasts about **3:30** and covers both **Auschwitz I** and **Auschwitz II-Birkenau**. Auschwitz I contains the museum exhibitions in the former prison blocks. Birkenau is the much larger site, with the railway entrance, surviving barracks and remains of the crematoria. A free museum shuttle links the two sites. Bags larger than 35 × 25 × 15 cm are not admitted.

Return to Krakow by train or direct bus after the visit.

## Day 3: Wieliczka

![Wieliczka Salt Mine](images/blog/krakow1.jpg)


Take a Koleje Małopolskie suburban train from Kraków Główny to **Wieliczka Rynek-Kopalnia**. The Daniłowicz Shaft entrance is approximately ten minutes on foot from the terminus.

Book the Tourist Route in the required language through [the mine's official site](https://www.wieliczka-saltmine.com/individual-tourist/tourist-route). The guided visit lasts **two to three hours** and includes **St Kinga's Chapel**, whose reliefs, statues and chandeliers were made using rock salt. The underground route also passes chambers, mining equipment and saline lakes. The route descends about 380 steps, contains more than 800 steps in total and returns to the surface by lift.
