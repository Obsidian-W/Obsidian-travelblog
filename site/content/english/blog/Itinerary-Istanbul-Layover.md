## Day 1: Sultanahmet

![Hagia Sophia](images/blog/istanbul2.jpg)

I had a 72-hour layover in Istanbul.

From **Istanbul Airport**, take the **M11 to Gayrettepe**, change to the **M2 towards Yenikapı** and get off at **Vezneciler**. Walk to **Laleli–Üniversite** for the **T1 tram to Sultanahmet**.

Begin at **Hagia Sophia**, then cross Sultanahmet Square to the **Blue Mosque**. Inside, look up at the tiled walls and the domes above the prayer hall. The former **Hippodrome** runs along the west side of the mosque: the Egyptian obelisk, Serpent Column and German Fountain are all on this walk.

The **Basilica Cistern** is back beside Hagia Sophia. Its underground walk passes rows of columns, including the two supported by carved Medusa heads. [Cistern admission with an audio guide is available here (affiliate link)](https://www.getyourguide.com/istanbul-l56/istanbul-basilica-cistern-skip-the-line-ticket-t698373/?partner_id=H1N6CGH&utm_medium=online_publisher).

Spend the afternoon at **Topkapı Palace**, behind Hagia Sophia. The courtyards lead towards the treasury, pavilions and terraces overlooking the Bosphorus. The **Harem** has tiled rooms, courtyards and the private apartments of the imperial household. Finish in **Gülhane Park** below the palace.

[Topkapı admission with an audio guide can be booked here (affiliate link)](https://www.getyourguide.com/istanbul-l56/topkapi-palace-fast-track-entry-every-15-minutes-t523510/?partner_id=H1N6CGH&utm_medium=online_publisher). Allow around **three hours** for the palace and Harem.

## Day 2: Galata

![Galata Tower](images/blog/istanbul3.jpg)

Take the T1 tram to **Eminönü** for the **Spice Bazaar** and **New Mosque**. The shops continue into the streets behind the bazaar, towards **Rüstem Pasha Mosque**, known for its blue İznik tiles.

Cross **Galata Bridge**, past the fishermen, and climb from Karaköy to **Galata Tower**. The streets around the tower have old apartment buildings, small shops and steep stairways down towards the port.


Continue along **İstiklal Avenue**, stopping at **St Anthony of Padua Church** and the **Çiçek Pasajı** arcade before reaching **Taksim Square**. The **F1 funicular** descends from Taksim to Kabataş, where the T1 tram returns to Sultanahmet.

## Day 3: Kadikoy

Take a public ferry from **Eminönü** or **Karaköy** to **Kadıköy**, around **0:20–0:30**. From the pier, walk into the market around **Güneşli Bahçe Street** for fish stalls, bakeries and places to eat. Continue through **Bahariye Street** to **Moda**, then follow the waterfront back towards the ferry terminal.

The ferry ride crosses between the European and Asian sides, with views back towards Topkapı and the mosques. Return to Eminönü or Karaköy for the airport connection. From Karaköy, the Tünel funicular leads to Şişhane on the M2, which connects with the airport's M11 at Gayrettepe.

## With more days

### Topkapı and the Archaeology Museums

Visit the **Istanbul Archaeology Museums**, beside Gülhane, for the **Alexander Sarcophagus**, ancient sculpture and the museum courtyards. The carved sarcophagi and galleries of finds from across the former Ottoman lands make this a substantial museum visit beside the palace district.

### Another ferry day

Take the ferry from **Eminönü to Üsküdar**, around **0:20**. Visit **Mihrimah Sultan Mosque** beside the pier and follow the waterfront north towards **Kuzguncuk**, with its wooden houses, small shops and side streets rising above the Bosphorus. The walk from Üsküdar to Kuzguncuk is roughly **three kilometres**; buses along the waterfront return to the ferry pier.
