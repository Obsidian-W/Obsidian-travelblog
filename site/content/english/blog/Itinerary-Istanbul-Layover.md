# Itinerary done quick

## Istanbul

| Day | Destination | Notes |
|:---:|:---|:---|
| Day 1 | [Sultanahmet](#day-1-sultanahmet) | Hagia Sophia, Blue Mosque and historic centre |
| Day 2 | [Galata](#day-2-galata) | Eminonu, Galata and Istiklal Avenue |
| Day 3 | [Kadikoy](#day-3-kadikoy) | Bosphorus ferry and Asian Istanbul |

## Day 1: Sultanahmet

![Hagia Sophia](images/blog/istanbul2.jpg)

I had a 72-hour layover, which left three days in the city. From Istanbul Airport, take the M11 metro and change for the city rail network towards **Sultanahmet**.

Start outside **Hagia Sophia** and the Blue Mosque, then cross Sultanahmet Square to the Hippodrome and the German Fountain. Add the Basilica Cistern if the queue leaves enough time.

## Day 2: Galata

![Galata Tower](images/blog/istanbul3.jpg)

Take the tram or walk downhill to Eminonu, cross **Galata Bridge** and climb to **Galata Tower**.


Continue up Istiklal Avenue to Taksim Square, then return by metro or the historic funicular towards the waterfront.

## Day 3: Kadikoy

Take a public ferry from Eminonu or Karakoy to **Kadikoy**. Walk through the market streets, continue to the Moda waterfront and return across the Bosphorus. Collect the luggage and use the metro connection back to Istanbul Airport.
