I landed in Abu Dhabi, transferred directly to Dubai, then returned to Abu Dhabi the following day for my flight. It worked, but it wasted a fair part of a 48-hour stopover. If the flights allow it, spend both days in one city. Dubai is the better choice for the old centre and a desert trip; Abu Dhabi for the mosque, museums and Corniche.

## Day 1: Dubai

![Al Fahidi in Dubai](images/blog/uae2.jpg)
![Desert trip outside Dubai](images/blog/uae1.jpg)

I took the airport transfer from Abu Dhabi straight to Dubai. In the morning, take the Green Line to **Sharaf DG** and walk into Al Fahidi Historical Neighbourhood. Continue through the wind-tower houses to the textile souk and **Bur Dubai Abra Station**. Route CR1 crosses Dubai Creek to Deira Old Souq; the fare checked in September 2026 was **AED 1 in cash**. From the landing, the spice souk and gold souk are beside each other. [Dubai RTA publishes the abra route and operating hours](https://rta.ae/wps/portal/rta/ae/home/promotion/new-marine-routes-abra).

After lunch, take the metro from Al Ras or Gold Souq towards **Burj Khalifa/Dubai Mall**. The covered walkway from the station is long. Dubai Mall, the aquarium window, the Burj Khalifa plaza and the fountain basin fill the early afternoon without adding another taxi ride.

Keep the late afternoon and evening for a pre-booked desert excursion. [This desert safari (affiliate link)](https://www.getyourguide.com/en-gb/dubai-l173/dubai-extreme-desert-safari-with-dinner-t89937/?partner_id=H1N6CGH&utm_medium=online_publisher) includes hotel pickup, dune driving, sandboarding, a short camel ride and dinner before the return to Dubai. Check that the hotel lies inside its pickup area before booking.

## Day 2: Abu Dhabi

![Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque](images/blog/uae3.jpg)

Take the E100 from Al Ghubaiba or the E101 from Ibn Battuta to **Abu Dhabi Central Bus Station**. Both are inter-emirate buses; [Abu Dhabi Mobility lists E100, E101 and E102 on the Dubai route](https://admobility.gov.ae/en/inter-emirates-services). I arrived around lunchtime.

From the bus station, walk along Al Falah Street to **Qasr Al Hosn**, then continue to the Founder's Memorial and the **Corniche**. The walk is about five kilometres. Bus 24 is the direct public connection from the Al Khalidiyah side of the centre to Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque.

Go to **Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque** in the late afternoon and stay through sunset, when the white marble and pools look completely different under the lights. Reserve the access pass on the [official mosque website](https://www.szgmc.gov.ae/en/individual-booking). The visitor hours checked in September 2026 were 09:00–20:30 Saturday to Thursday and 09:00–11:30 plus 14:30–20:30 on Fridays.

For an evening flight, pre-book Careem or Uber from the mosque to Zayed International Airport and use the pickup point shown in the app. The airport identifies both as official ride-hailing services with designated collection areas.
