## Day 1: Old Town

![The Powder Tower and Municipal House](images/blog/prague1.jpg)

Start at the **Powder Tower** and Municipal House, then follow Celetná to **Old Town Square**. Visit the Old Town Hall and its tower before walking north into Josefov.

The **Prague Jewish Town** ticket covers the **Maisel**, **Pinkas**, **Spanish** and **Old-New synagogues**, plus the **Old Jewish Cemetery**. Allow around two to three hours for the circuit. Pinkas contains a memorial to the Czech and Moravian victims of the Holocaust; the cemetery's tightly packed headstones rise above generations of burials. These sites close on Saturdays and Jewish holidays.

Continue towards **Klementinum** and **Charles Bridge**, cross to Kampa and return along the east bank past the National Theatre. The complete route is about six kilometres before time inside the monuments. ## Day 2: Prague Castle

![Prague from above](images/blog/prague2.jpg)

Take tram 22 to **Pražský hrad** and enter the castle through the Second Courtyard. The main visitor circuit covers **St Vitus Cathedral**, the **Old Royal Palace**, **St George's Basilica** and **Golden Lane**. In the cathedral, look for the stained-glass windows and the decorated **St Wenceslas Chapel**. The Old Royal Palace contains **Vladislav Hall**, with its ribbed vaulting; Golden Lane is a row of small houses inside the fortifications. In September, the historical buildings are open from 09:00 to 17:00; Sunday entry to the cathedral begins at noon.

[A guided Prague Castle visit takes around 2:30 and includes the main circuit (affiliate link)](https://www.getyourguide.com/prague-l10/prague-castle-small-group-tour-with-local-guide-admission-t219531/?partner_id=H1N6CGH&utm_medium=online_publisher).

Leave through the eastern gate, pass the old castle steps and walk down through Malá Strana to the Lennon Wall and Kampa. Cross the Legion Bridge into the New Town for the rotating Kafka head and Wenceslas Square.

## Day 3: Troja

![Troja Chateau](images/blog/prague3.jpg)

Take Metro C to **Nádraží Holešovice**, then bus 112 or 234 to **Zoo Praha–Troja**. Troja Chateau is beside the bus stop. The exterior staircase is covered in sculptures; inside, the large painted hall and ceiling depict the Habsburg dynasty. The rooms and formal garden take about **1:30**.

The botanical garden begins uphill behind the chateau. Enter through the southern gate for the vineyard and St Claire's Chapel, then continue into the outdoor collections or the Fata Morgana greenhouse. Bus 234 stops at **Botanická zahrada Troja**, which avoids walking back down to the zoo entrance.

For the return, take the bus to **Nádraží Holešovice** and Metro C back into the centre.

The chateau closes outside its April-to-October season. In September 2026, the adult ticket was **CZK 200**.

## With more days

### A full day in the castle district

Start at **Strahov Monastery**, above Malá Strana, for the decorated library halls and the view over the city from its terraces. Walk through **Pohořelec** and the small lanes of **Nový Svět**, then return south to **Petřín**. The hill has wooded paths, gardens and a lookout tower above the river; descend towards **Újezd** for the tram back into the centre.


### Vyšehrad and the south bank

Take Metro C to **Vyšehrad** and walk into the old fortress. Follow the ramparts above the Vltava to the **Basilica of Saints Peter and Paul** and the cemetery, where Dvořák and Smetana are buried. Descend towards the river and walk north along **Náplavka** to the **Dancing House**. Allow half a day for the fortress and riverside walk.
