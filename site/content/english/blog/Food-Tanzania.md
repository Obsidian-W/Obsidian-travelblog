## Ugali and everyday inland food

![Ugali](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:Redirect/file/Ugali%2020251129%20163844.jpg)

**Ugali** is a stiff maize-flour porridge with a mild flavour, served with beans, greens or meat sauce. It is eaten by hand with the sauce or other accompaniments.

Inland, around Arusha, Moshi, Dodoma and Mwanza, you will eat more maize, beans, plantains, meat and greens. On the coast and Zanzibar, expect more rice, coconut, spices and seafood.

**Maharage** are beans in sauce, while **sukuma wiki** are cooked greens, usually collards or kale. **Makande** is maize and beans cooked together: basic, heavy and filling.

**Chipsi mayai** is an omelette cooked with chips, usually served with tomato or chilli sauce. It is cheap and filling.

**Ndizi nyama**, found around Kilimanjaro, is a meat stew made with green cooking bananas. They are starchy, not sweet, and work more like potatoes.

## Arusha and Moshi

**Nyama choma** is grilled meat, often ordered by piece or weight. It usually comes with **kachumbari**, a chopped tomato, onion and chilli salad, with chips, ugali or rice available on the side.

## Dar es Salaam

In Dar, **mishkaki** are small skewers of marinated meat cooked over charcoal and eaten with chilli sauce. **Bhajia** are gram-flour fritters; samosas and chapati are also everywhere.

Fish is served grilled or fried, with accompaniments such as rice, beans and coconut sauce.

**Kariakoo Market** sells fresh produce and snacks.

## Zanzibar and the Swahili coast

On Zanzibar, I took a **spice-farm tour** and enjoyed the smells and samples. It was touristy, but worth the half day; I happened to be the only visitor on my tour.

![Spice market in Stone Town](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:Redirect/file/Spice%20Market%20in%20Stone%20town%20Zanzibar.JPG)

On Zanzibar, **pilau** and **biryani** are two ways of preparing spiced rice. Pilau cooks everything into the same seasoned broth. Biryani combines rice with a separately cooked, more heavily spiced meat or fish mixture.

**Octopus curry** and **wali wa nazi**, rice cooked with coconut milk, are coastal dishes. **Darajani Market** sells produce and spices.

**Urojo**, or Zanzibar mix, is a thick, tangy soup loaded with potato, fritters, chutney, chilli and other toppings chosen by the seller.

**Zanzibar pizza** has very little to do with pizza. It is thin dough folded around meat, egg, vegetables or a sweet filling, then fried on a hot plate.

**Vitumbua** are small coconut rice cakes cooked in round moulds. **Mandazi** are pieces of fried dough served with tea and are best when freshly cooked.

I ate at **Forodhani Gardens** in Stone Town on my **2023 trip** and found it overrated. A small local restaurant served much better food, and I went back for most of my meals afterwards.

## Coffee, juice and alcohol

Tanzania grows coffee around Kilimanjaro and in other highland areas. Coffee-farm visits are available in the Chagga villages near Moshi.

Fresh passion fruit, mango and tamarind juices are widely available, alongside drinks made from diluted syrup. Avocado juice is particularly thick and filling.
