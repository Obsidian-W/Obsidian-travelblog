## Hey, its Obsidian

Also known as Timothé Paris, Writer & Traveler extraordinaire.
I made his blog to share my trips and help those that need intel on how to travel some less common destinations.
Hence in this 100% free blog, you will find tips, itineraries, info on transportation and some articles on less-than-common places.

This is an independently published blog. Advertising does not influence the destinations I cover or what I write about them.

## Contact

If you have a question about an itinerary, a destination, or a possible collaboration, send me a message here.

I read every message, but I would rather receive a few specific details than a vague request. Tell me where you want to go, what kind of trip you have in mind, and what stage of planning you are at.
